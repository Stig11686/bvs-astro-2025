<Section tone="tint" heading="Compliant *by default*.">

Every dental website I build includes the compliance requirements your practice needs:

- **GDC number display:** every registered dentist's GDC number prominently placed as required
- **CQC registration and rating:** displayed correctly for CQC-registered practices
- **GDPR-compliant contact and appointment forms:** patient data handled correctly from day one
- **Cookie consent:** properly implemented, not a banner ticked off a checklist
- **Privacy policy:** written for healthcare, not generic boilerplate

Most generic web designers don't know these requirements exist. I build them in as standard.

</Section>

Your website should be your best receptionist: professional, helpful, and always available.
