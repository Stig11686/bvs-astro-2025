## A New Saga Begins

Viking is a family-run sports retailer in Silsden, West Yorkshire. For ten years they've fitted cricketers from the local park to the international arena — same shop, same people, same hands-on advice.

Now they've opened a second front. Hockey.

This wasn't a cautious toe in the water. It was a complete new brand, a complete new range, and a complete new shop — built from a blank canvas. Same trusted team; a whole new game.

## What I Built

A full e-commerce launch — shop, brand and buying guide — designed to look ten years old on day one.

### A Complete Shop From Scratch

- **Own-brand Norse line-up** — Warrior, Odin, Thor and Valhalla sticks from £35 to £190, carrying the same identity as the cricket range's Valkyrie heritage
- **Clear categories** — sticks, luggage and accessories, easy to browse and buy
- **Mobile-friendly** — players can shop from the sideline or the sofa

### An In-Store Fitter's Buying Guide

The counter advice, put online — so buyers choose the right stick with confidence:

- **Stick length by height** — a proper sizing table, not guesswork
- **Carbon percentage explained** — matching power and forgiveness to a player's level
- **Bow profiles & positions** — mid bow for control, low bow for 3D skills and drag-flicks
- **Weight, grip & care** — how to pick balance and keep a stick match-ready

### A Range Shaped by Customers

- **Built-in feedback** — customers tell Viking what to stock, and the range grows around real demand
- **Honest positioning** — a new, deliberately small line-up presented as a strength, not an apology

### Self-Managed & Scalable

Built on WordPress and Elementor — the same setup Jeff already runs for cricket:

- **Owner-managed** — add products, prices and stock without a developer
- **Ready to expand** — goalie sticks and new bags already lined up
- **Linked to the cricket business** — one trusted name, two ranges

## Looking Ahead

This is the first line-up, not the finished team.

With the shop live and the foundations set, Viking is building the range around what customers ask for — goalie sticks and new luggage are next, with more to follow. Hockey now stands alongside the established cricket business under one trusted, family-run name.

## Key Takeaways

For any business launching into a brand-new category:

1. **Expertise travels** — ten years of fitting kit is an asset in any sport, if you put it online
2. **Show your working** — a proper buying guide builds trust a product grid never will
3. **Let customers shape the range** — a feedback loop turns a small launch into a strength
4. **Own your platform** — build on something the owner can run day-to-day
