## Two Venues, One Address

66 Sheep Street is a bakery by day and a bistro by night, with a small bar, a Sunday roast and an upstairs room for private dining. In the morning there's coffee and pastries from the hatch on Sheep Street. By Thursday evening it's candlelight, cocktails and a proper dinner menu.

It's a lovely idea. It's also a hard one to explain on a phone screen in five seconds.

And it was brand new. No old website to improve, no reviews to lean on, no regulars yet. Whatever went live had to make a venue that hadn't opened look like somewhere worth booking.

**So I built them a website that lets every visitor find their version of 66 straight away — and book a table from any page.**

A website that explains the whole place clearly, and then points people to the bit they came for.

### A Front Door That Splits Two Ways

- **One line to explain it** — "Bakery by Day. Bistro by Night. We Roast on Sundays." Everything a first-time visitor needs to know, before they scroll
- **"Choose your moment"** — the homepage sends people straight to Bakery & Brunch or the Bistro, rather than making them work it out
- **The hatch gets its moment** — the street-facing takeaway window is part of the story, so it's part of the site

### A Page for Every Reason to Visit

- **Bakery & Brunch, Bistro and Sunday Lunch** — each with its own page, its own menu and its own reason to come
- **Private Hire & Events** — a dedicated page for the upstairs dining room, for birthdays, celebrations and business dinners
- **About, Gallery and Contact** — the people, the space and the practical details, all where you'd expect them

### Booking in One Tap

- **"Book a table" on every page** — in the header, on the homepage and throughout, going straight to the venue's booking system
- **Tap-to-call phone number** — for anyone who'd rather just ring
- **Find us without hunting** — address, map and opening hours on the homepage and in the footer

### Built Alongside the Marketing Team

- **One look across everything** — working with the marketing and social media team so the site matches the brand, photography and tone they were creating
- **Socials connected** — Instagram, Facebook and TikTok linked throughout
- **Newsletter sign-up** — so people who find 66 online have a reason to hear from them again

### Ready for Opening

- **Policy pages in place** — privacy, cookies, terms and allergen information ready at launch
- **Simple to update** — built in WordPress and Elementor so the team can change menus, hours and news without waiting on a developer

## Key Takeaways

For anyone opening a new restaurant, café or bar:

1. **Your website is your first review** — before anyone has eaten with you, it's the only thing they have to judge you on
2. **Let people choose their visit** — if you do more than one thing, give each its own page and its own route in
3. **Put booking everywhere** — don't make someone go looking for the button once they've decided
4. **Build it with your marketing, not after it** — one brand across the website and socials looks far more established than two
