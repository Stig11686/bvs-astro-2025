<Accordion label="Why is my website getting traffic but no enquiries?">

The most common causes are: no clear call to action, a broken contact form, phone number and contact details that are hard to find, lack of trust signals like reviews or testimonials, and copy that talks about the business rather than the customer's problem. A website audit will identify exactly which of these apply to your site.

</Accordion>

<Accordion label="How do I know if my website is generating leads?">

If you don't have Google Analytics set up with goal tracking, you won't have a clear picture. At minimum, you should know how many people visit your site each month and how many submit a contact form or call you from it. If you can't answer those questions, setting up basic tracking should be a priority.

</Accordion>

<Accordion label="Do I need a new website or can my current one be fixed?">

It depends on the site. Some websites need targeted fixes: better calls to action, a working contact form, clearer navigation. Others have such fundamental structural or technical problems that a rebuild is more cost-effective than trying to patch them. An audit will tell you which category you're in.

</Accordion>
