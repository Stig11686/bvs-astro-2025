<Section eyebrow="Why this matters" heading="A cheap website is the *most expensive* kind">

You can get a website for a few hundred pounds. Plenty of people will knock one out from a template over a weekend. The problem turns up later: it doesn't rank, it doesn't convert, it's a pain to change, and inside a year you're paying someone else to start again. The cheap site cost you twice, plus a year of enquiries you never got.

Your website is the hardest-working member of your team. It sells while you sleep, qualifies people before they call, and decides whether a stranger trusts you in the first five seconds. *Build it as an afterthought and it'll work like one.*

That's the difference in how I build. Every site starts with what your business needs to achieve: the enquiries, the bookings, the searches you want to be found for. The design follows from there. It looks great because it should. But good looks alone won't bring the work in. *The goal is a site that pays for itself.*

</Section>
