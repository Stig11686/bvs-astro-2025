Ilkley Dental Care is a well-established practice that needed a modern website to match their quality of patient care. Their old site had a hidden menu, difficult navigation, and a broken contact form they didn't know about.

**The problems:**

- The contact form wasn't working, so they were losing enquiries
- The menu only appeared after scrolling, which confused visitors
- The phone number was buried, with no clear call to action
- Staff found it hard to update, so it was often out of date
- Google reviews were being collected twice with manual input

**What I fixed:**

- Rebuilt the contact form
- Phone number prominent at the top of every page
- Clear "Book Appointment" buttons throughout
- Simplified navigation, with key pages visible immediately
- An automated Google reviews widget, so no more manual work
- An easy-to-update WordPress CMS for staff

**The result:** Now generating 30+ enquiries per month from the same traffic. The team believes in their website now.
